## 2. Core Thesis

STEAK is not a coin about crypto staking — it is a coin about the oldest, most visceral staking ritual in human history: you raise something with your own hands, and then you harvest what you built. The viral moment (@lazefrito, ~1M views) is the founding myth: one person bottle-fed a calf, raised it with love, and ate it. That tension — care → commitment → harvest — maps perfectly onto DeFi staking in a way no "stake your tokens" project has ever captured. The community is not degens; they are **Ranchers** who lock their STEAK to raise the price, then harvest the rewards. Every meme, every airdrop, every governance vote reinforces this single lore loop.

## 3. Token Mechanics

### Supply
- **Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 STEAK (1 billion — "a whole herd")
- **Naming:** 1 STEAK = 1 oz. Fractions called "cuts" (ribeye, sirloin, brisket tiers).
- **No mint authority after launch** — supply is fixed at birth, like a calf you name.

### Allocation
| Bucket | % | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Public liquidity pool | 40% | Burned LP tokens, locked forever |
| Staking rewards vault ("The Pasture") | 30% | Released only via staking emissions |
| Founding Rancher airdrop | 10% | To holders who mint the Genesis NFT within 72h |
| Team / dev ("The Ranch Hand") | 10% | 12-month linear vest, cliff at 3 months |
| Community treasury ("The Barn") | 10% | Governed by stakers |

### Staking — "The Pasture"
Staking is not a generic APY box. It is a **ranching commitment mechanic**:

- **Lock periods are called "Grazing Seasons":**
  - 🐄 **Calf** (7 days) — 1x multiplier, no early withdrawal
  - 🐂 **Yearling** (30 days) — 2x multiplier
  - 🥩 **Prime Cut** (90 days) — 4x multiplier
  - 🔥 **Dry-Aged** (180 days) — 8x multiplier + governance rights

- **Early unstake = "Slaughter Tax":** 25% penalty burned, 5% redistributed to remaining stakers. The penalty is a meme: "You raised it, you left it. That's not ranching. That's abandonment." This framing turns a standard penalty into shareable content.

- **Emission schedule:** Emissions halve every 90 days ("Seasons"). Season 1 is the fattest rewards period — designed to reward early Ranchers who lock in before the viral moment fades.

### Airdrops — "The Harvest"
Airdrops are called **Harvests** and are tied directly to the staking narrative:

1. **Genesis Harvest (Day 1–3):** Airdrop to anyone who takes a selfie with a steak (raw or cooked), posts to X with `$STEAK` and `#BottleFedToBlockchain`, and mints the free Genesis Rancher NFT. NFT = 1,000 STEAK. Cap: 10,000 NFTs = 10M STEAK deployed from treasury.

2. **Loyalty Harvest (weekly):** Every Sunday ("Butcher Day"), 1% of the Barn treasury is distributed pro-rata to all active Dry-Aged stakers. Automated, on-chain. Called out publicly every week as a social event.

3. **Viral Harvest (event-triggered):** If a post with `#STEAK` hits 100K impressions in 24h, a snapshot is taken and a bonus drop goes to every wallet that interacted with STEAK contracts that week. Rewards virality directly and creates a game out of posting.

### Genesis NFT — "The Calf Tag"
- Free mint (gas only) for first 72 hours
- A simple branded ear-tag image (calf → steer → steak progression based on staking tier)
- NFT upgrades visually as the holder moves through Grazing Seasons — the calf grows into a prime cut
- Non-transferable for 30 days (you raised it, you own it)

## 5. Why This Angle Wins

**The lore is pre-existing and authenticated.** Most meme coins manufacture a narrative after the fact. STEAK has a real, documented, emotionally polarizing viral moment as its origin. The original post exists. The views exist. The founder is real and identifiable. That is extraordinarily rare and it collapses the "is this legit?" skepticism that kills most launches.

**The narrative pun is load-bearing, not decorative.** "Staking" and "steak" is not just a ticker joke — the entire mechanic (raise → commit → harvest → reward) maps onto DeFi staking so cleanly that the product explains itself through the meme. Users understand why they should lock tokens without reading docs because they already understand ranching.

**The community identity is a keeper, not a flipper.** Calling holders "Ranchers" and framing early exits as abandonment creates social pressure to hold. The Slaughter Tax is both a financial mechanism and a meme that mocks paper hands — the community will enforce this norm organically.

**The viral window is now.** The original post is ~1M views and recent. In crypto, a meme coin launched within days of the viral event it references captures the attention pipeline. Every person who saw that post and thought "this is insane" is a potential buyer if they see the coin within the next week. That window closes fast.

**The emotional range is unusual.** The original story is simultaneously funny, disturbing, wholesome, and brutal. That emotional complexity makes it shareable across audiences who normally don't overlap — farmers, crypto degens, vegans dunking on it, foodies, and people who just think it's a wild story. Wide emotional surface area = wide distribution.

## 6. Key Risks

### Market Risks
- **Pump/dump velocity:** With a viral launch, sniper bots and early flippers will attempt to dump before genuine community forms. Mitigation: anti-sniper tax for first 30 minutes post-launch (5% buy tax that burns, removed after block 100 of trading). Not a perfect fix but raises cost for snipers.
- **Dev token unlock pressure:** The 10% team allocation vesting cliff at 3 months will create sell pressure signals. Publish the vesting contract address publicly on day 1 so the community can monitor it themselves.
- **Short viral shelf life:** The original post's virality has a half-life. Every day between the viral peak and launch costs attention. The 72-hour GTM is aggressive by design — if launch slips to week 2, recalibrate expectations significantly.

### Narrative Risks
- **Animal ethics backlash:** The story will attract organized backlash from vegan and animal rights communities on X. This is predictable. Do not engage defensively. The correct play: let the community respond with jokes, do not let the founder engage with outrage bait. Backlash generates impressions. Impressions generate buyers. A PR crisis in crypto is marketing unless the founder says something genuinely harmful.
- **"It's just a meme coin" dismissal:** Serious crypto investors will dismiss this. That's fine — this product is not for them at launch. The risk is if the founder tries to chase legitimacy by pivoting messaging to institutional framing. Don't. Commit to the bit.
- **Copycat launches:** Within 48 hours of a successful launch, copycats will appear (STEAKX, STEAKINU, BEEFCOIN). Mitigation: the founder's authenticated identity and the Genesis NFT tied to the original story are the moat. The real story can't be forked.

### Compliance Flags (brief, not legal advice)
- Airdrops tied to social posting actions may constitute a promotional securities offering in some jurisdictions — consult counsel on airdrop eligibility criteria before launch.
- Staking rewards with fixed APY projections may be characterized as investment contracts under Howey in the US — frame as "emissions from the protocol" not "yield" or "interest."
- If the founder is US-based, KYC/AML considerations apply to any token sale structure. The free Genesis NFT mint (gas only) is lower risk than a presale or private round.
- Do not sell tokens to the public via a website that geolocates US users without legal review.
