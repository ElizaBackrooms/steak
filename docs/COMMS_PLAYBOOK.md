# STEAK Communications Playbook

**For:** @lazefrito, mods, community amplifiers
**Purpose:** what to say, what not to say, and templates you can copy-paste tonight.
**Voice:** rancher deadpan. Confident, unhurried, funny, never desperate. The joke works because we don't oversell it.
**Not legal or financial advice.**

---

## 1. Voice rules

**Do:**
- Sound like a rancher who accidentally became a crypto founder, not the reverse.
- Use short sentences. Full stops. Cowboy cadence.
- Meme in public. Reply-guy with jokes, not with charts.
- Cite receipts (Solscan, Raydium, pump.fun) more than you talk.
- Repeat the origin story in your own words when asked. Never scripted.

**Don't:**
- Never say "guaranteed," "risk-free," "will 100x," "safu," "gem," "next SHIB," "moon soon," "we're going to Binance."
- Never DM anyone a link first. Ever.
- Never post a price target.
- Never delete a tweet. Correct in reply.
- Never dunk on vegans or animal-rights posters personally. Let the community respond. Do not engage outrage bait as founder.
- Never talk about your unrealized P&L publicly.
- Never say "this is not a rug." That phrase reads as suspicious. Show, don't swear.

## 5. Reply templates — FUD

Copy-paste with 2-second edits. Always link a transparency artifact.

### "This is a rug"

```
Fair concern. Here's what I can show you:

- LP: burned at graduation → <Raydium/burn tx link>
- Team vesting contract (I can't touch these for 3 months): <link>
- Every treasury wallet, all of them, publicly labeled: <link to TOKENOMICS.md #7>

If I rug I'm doing it in front of a million people with my real face. Not a great plan.
```

### "Dev is dumping / whale wallet at top holders is you"

```
That wallet is <label>. It's public on the tokenomics doc. I hold <X>%, address <addr>. Watch it. If it moves without a 72h notice on this account, screenshot me and dunk.
```

### "This is dead / MC dropping"

```
Season 1 is 90 days. Butcher Day is every Sunday. If you're checking the chart at hour 4, you're not the target audience. Take a walk. Come back Sunday.
```

### "You're glorifying animal cruelty"

Do not reply as founder. Let the community joke. If you must respond (once), keep it flat:

```
The original post is about raising an animal with care and eating it. That's how food works for most of human history. I'm not going to argue about ethics on X. If it's not for you, don't buy.
```

Then mute. Do not thread. Do not quote-tweet the accuser.

### "You didn't disclose X"

Immediately disclose X in a public reply with a link. Never argue about disclosure. Being wrong-in-public and fixing-in-public builds trust faster than being defensive.

### "Insider pre-mint / snipe accusations"

```
Top 10 wallets, labeled and public: <link>. If any of them are me and I didn't disclose it, I'll airdrop my full founder allocation to the community pro-rata. That's the standing offer.
```

Only make this offer if you can honestly stand behind it. It is a nuclear-grade transparency move — do not bluff.

### "Where's the utility?"

```
Utility is: you stake it, you earn from it, you vote with it, and you get Harvests if you stick around. The rest is a story. Story is the point. If you need more than that, this isn't for you.
```

## 8. What to escalate to the founder personally

Mods can handle most things. Escalate to @lazefrito personally only for:

- Any accusation with on-chain evidence (a screenshot of a wallet that looks like a rug).
- A large media outlet (>100k reach) asking for a quote.
- Any legal / cease-and-desist / subpoena / regulator email.
- Any wallet-compromise, key-leak, or admin-panel-breach event.
- A partnership offer that involves > 1% of Barn treasury or > 1M STEAK.

Everything else: mods handle. Founder posts memes.

## 9. Cadence — first 72h posting rhythm

- Hours 0–6: post or reply every ~10–15 min.
- Hours 6–24: post or reply every ~30 min. Never > 60 min silent.
- Hours 24–48: post 6–10 times per day. At least one long-form (story or tokenomics).
- Hours 48–72: 4–6 posts per day. Countdown to Genesis snapshot every 6 hours.

After 72h, settle into: 1 story post/day, 1 stats post/day, 1 meme/day, 1 Butcher Day recap per week.

## 10. Words that get you rate-limited or shadowbanned

Avoid or replace:

- "Guaranteed" → "designed to"
- "Risk-free" → do not use this at all
- "Yield / interest" → "emissions / rewards"
- "Presale" → do not run one, do not use the word
- "Airdrop" in the pinned tweet → the algorithm sometimes throttles it; use "Harvest" as the primary word, "airdrop" once in the explanation

## 11. If everything goes wrong

If the launch is dying (hour 6 and volume is dead), do NOT:
- Buy your own token more.
- Panic-post that "we're not dead."
- Beg for retweets.
- Blame anyone.

Do:
- Post one honest thread: "Slow start. Herd's small. Butcher Day still on Sunday. If you're still here, you're the herd." That vulnerability sometimes flips the trajectory. If it doesn't, you had a small launch, and small launches with real transparency turn into medium launches over weeks.

If the launch is going nuclear (100x in 6h), do NOT:
- Sell any founder tokens.
- Promise anything you can't deliver.
- Announce a CEX listing you haven't confirmed.
- Do a Spaces immediately — you'll say something you regret.

Do:
- Post the LP burn tx again.
- Repin the transparency wallet list.
- Announce the Genesis snapshot time again, more prominently.
- Take a 20-minute walk. Then post.
